Our Story

Bringing India's
Festive Spirit to the UK

Founded in the heart of Sheffield, Festive India was born from a passion for authentic Indian culture. We saw a gap — a community looking for genuine, beautifully crafted festival wear without compromise on quality.

Every piece in our collection is handpicked from skilled artisans across India — weavers in Varanasi, jewellers in Rajasthan, and craftspeople across Gujarat and Bengal. We carry their stories, traditions, and expertise to your doorstep.

Whether you're preparing for Garba, Diwali, Eid, a wedding, or any cultural celebration, we're here to make sure you look and feel extraordinary.
